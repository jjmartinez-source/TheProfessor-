Teacher guide

How this SAQ Gizmo works

Content: SAQ 1 examines overland, maritime, and desert trade routes (AMSCO Unit 2). SAQ 2 compares Song China (Unit 1) and Ottoman governance (Unit 3). All parts can be answered using only the on-screen source packet.

Points: 1 point is available for each part, 3 for each SAQ, 6 total. A student should make a clear historical claim and explain how the passage supports it. Some questions intentionally require distinct examples as a higher-evidence classroom target.

Grading: The downloadable off-line HTML uses a transparent key-idea matcher. It can miss valid synonyms or reward an answer with the right words but weak reasoning. Its estimate should be reviewed by a teacher. When deployed to Netlify with OPENAI_API_KEY, the included server-side function can issue live model-generated feedback; its scores remain advisory.

Literacy: Students receive writing advice separately from content points. Minor grammar or spelling errors do not automatically remove historical credit.

Exam authenticity: The 2027 AP World History exam uses three source-based SAQs (one secondary-text source, one primary-text source, and one nontextual source). This is a focused source-only classroom exercise, not a replica of the complete exam. The passages are clearly attributed to the course textbook and are not falsely presented as historical eyewitness quotations.

References: AMSCO pp. 3–9, 77–84, 95–110, 127–133, and 158–162. College Board AP World History: Modern Exam and scoring guidelines.
